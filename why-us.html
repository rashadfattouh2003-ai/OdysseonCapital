Honest About It

Strengths

  • The exit is written before the entry, which removes the failure mode that costs self-directed investors the most.
  • Per-position published reporting that no regional competitor offers.
  • A full 2025 backtest across 2,049 trades with a 2.63 per cent maximum drawdown.
  • Two closed positions, both profitable, both documented to the fill.

Weaknesses

  • Two closed positions is not a track record by any institutional standard.
  • One strategy, so a regime without volatility compression suppresses the whole book.
  • No distribution and no referral network.
  • The option overlay carries far more variance than the spot strategy beneath it.

Opportunities

  • Nobody in the regional set treats transparency as the product rather than an obligation.
  • ADGM has lowered the practical cost of being a licensed manager, and over 110 now operate there.
  • Investors who shopped for a manager and stayed put are the warmest segment, and nobody speaks to them.

Threats

  • Allocators who screen on three years cannot be converted at any price this year.
  • The transparency advantage is copyable by a larger manager with a decade of history behind it.
  • An early drawdown materially worse than 2.63 per cent, in public, with no history to absorb it.
