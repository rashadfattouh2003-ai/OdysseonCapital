Introduction

The Position

Paradium.AI trades on NYSE American as PAAI. Until 31 August 2026 the same company traded as The Arena Group under AREN. The desk bought it in the first week of September and sold the whole position on 18 September.

Our Logic

Through mid August the stock carried real volume off the rebrand from The Arena Group to Paradium.AI. The move faded and the price came back to ninety cents, where the downside was close to spent.

In front of that floor sat a calendar: the Elcano partnership pushing brand IP across programmatic channels, and the Lake Street BIG10 Conference in New York.

The catalyst that paid was larger. On 17 September Paradium signed a ten year platform agreement with Roundtable worth roughly one billion dollars, near one hundred million a year, against a company the market valued at about fifty million. Ten years of billing at twenty times the whole company. Roundtable would take 49.5% of the stock and pay $3.80 a share for it. We had paid ninety six cents.
