Introduction

The Position

Iovance Biotherapeutics makes Amtagvi, the first tumour-infiltrating lymphocyte therapy the FDA has ever approved, cleared in February 2024 for advanced melanoma.

Our Plan of Action

The trade began in mid‑March 2026; the entry price was just above $3.50. The target mapped out was $10.00 by January 2027 at the latest. Iovance, however, moved sooner rather than later, reaching the mid‑nines by August. It was time to begin scaling out, as the price prediction had virtually been achieved. Across August the position was averaged out, selling in a range from $9.36 down to $8.30, five months ahead of the date in the plan.

We exited for two reasons. The first was mechanical: holding past the move meant paying option decay every week for a target we had already effectively reached. The second was discipline. We took the trade we had written down, not the one hindsight would have written for us. Contentment, not greed.

The Result

The fund committed $50,407 of principal to this position. It closed the period at $148,706, taking the principal to 295.0 per cent of what went in, a gain of $98,299.

The position is not entirely closed. $7,000 of the proceeds stays in options as a tail, just over seven per cent of the gain and an amount we are willing to lose in full. It is held against the very real chance that Iovance clears $10.00 and keeps going.

Going Forward

The Algorithm

From here, we intend to run our own algorithm, and we scale into it as it earns the allocation. The algorithm function is fairly simple. It reads a name across multiple timeframes at once and waits for volatility to compress. A stretch of sideways movement and quiet is usually a coil, not an ending. When that compression shows up on both the short and long views simultaneously, and trend and momentum agree on direction, we take the position and define the exit before we’re in it. Where conviction is highest, we express the trade in an option.
