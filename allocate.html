One per cent a year, and twelve per cent of what we make for you above a twelve per cent hurdle. In a year we do not clear the hurdle, the twelve per cent is not charged.

The Process

Four Steps

Nothing moves until you have seen the strategy, the record and the terms, and asked whatever you need to ask.

01

A conversation

Fifteen to thirty minutes. We walk through the strategy, the two closed positions and the terms. No documents required.

02

Suitability

We establish whether a concentrated momentum strategy fits the role this capital plays for you. If it does not, we say so.

03

Mandate

Terms are agreed in writing and the account is set up. You see the positions and the marks as we do.

Reporting

Every closed position is written up and published in the format you can already read on the Ledger. At close, not at quarter end.
