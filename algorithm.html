The Product

What It Is

Squeeze Breakout V2 is a systematic momentum strategy. It is the engine behind the mandates Odysseon runs, and it is the reason a client allocates to us rather than picking names themselves.

The function is deliberately simple. It reads a name across multiple timeframes at once and waits for volatility to compress. A stretch of sideways movement and quiet is usually a coil, not an ending. When that compression shows up on both the short and long views simultaneously, and trend and momentum agree on direction, the algorithm takes the position and defines the exit before it is in it.

Nothing in that sequence requires an opinion. The entry is a condition, the size is a rule, and the exit is written at the same moment as the entry. That is the whole point: the strategy does not get to change its mind halfway through a position, and neither do we.

02

The Signal

Pre-Trade

What follows is the sequence the algorithm runs before any position is opened. It loops, so you can watch it as many times as you need.

It scans the universe, isolates the names where volatility has compressed on more than one timeframe, confirms that trend and momentum point the same way, checks the position against the risk budget, and only then writes the order. The exit sits in the order ticket alongside the entry. If any one of those checks fails, nothing is sent.

04

The Overlay

Where We Add Risk

The default plan trades spot. To extend the gains, and the risk, the trading team hand picks the strongest of those spot signals and takes the position as an option instead.

This is the only discretionary step in the process, and it is a step about expression, not about direction. The algorithm decides what to buy and when to leave. The team decides, on a small number of the highest conviction signals, whether to express that same decision with leverage. The two trades in the Ledger were both taken this way.

05

The Service

How Clients Access It

Odysseon runs the strategy as a managed mandate. The client allocates, we trade it, and the client sees the same positions and the same marks we do.

Capital is not pooled into a product the client cannot see through. Reporting is the same format as the Ledger on this site: every closed position written up with the entries, the exit, the figures and the reasoning, published when the money is booked rather than at the end of a quarter.
